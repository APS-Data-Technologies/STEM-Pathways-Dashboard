# Five dashboard questions and checked answers

These are the five questions defined in `product-plan.md`. Each answer below identifies the exact view and filters. Values were independently reconciled to the raw IPEDS files in `qa/full-source-check.json`. Sponsor approval of the question set remains open.

## How have Illinois STEM awards changed over time

Overview; all fields, all degrees, all cities and all institution types; 2016–2023. Annual awards rise from **22,744 to 25,243**, an **11.0%** endpoint increase and **1.5%** compound annual growth. The selected-period total is **187,386 awards**. This does not imply continuous growth in every intervening year.

## Which STEM fields grew fastest

STEM fields; same complete selection; sort by percent change. Computer & information sciences increases from **7,107 to 9,841**, or **38.5%**. Mathematics & statistics follows at **27.6%**. The 2020 classification break and new programs limit a causal interpretation of these changes.

## How do the degree levels differ

Overview; all fields; 2016–2023; all cities and types. The degree-level chart shows **108,418 bachelor's**, **63,197 master's** and **15,771 associate awards**. Change the degree filter to compare each level's annual trend. The bar chart totals cover all selected years.

## Which institutions award the most in a selected field

Overview; Computer & information sciences; Bachelor's; 2023 only; all cities and types. **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign** leads with **583 awards**. The ranking measures volume in this selection. Select its name to inspect its profile. Institution names use the latest name within the selected records.

## What is one institution's STEM profile

Institutions; Judson University; all fields, degrees, cities and types; 2016–2023. Judson has **66 awards** in scope. Both endpoint years have **6 awards**, producing **0.0%** endpoint change. This is a narrow STEM definition and excludes certificates and doctorates.

## Additional local advising example

Overview; Computer & information sciences; Bachelor's; 2023 only; Chicago; Public. The result is **566 awards across three institutions**. Compare Chicago State University (**9**) and University of Illinois Chicago (**454**). Northeastern Illinois University accounts for the remaining **103**. The comparison CSV for the first two contains two rows totaling **463**.

## Findings suitable for the demo

The five answers above provide five checked findings. Do not describe them as student pathways, employment demand or program-quality rankings. Every chart counts degrees awarded. New programs, reporting changes, institutional changes and NCES imputations can affect the series.
