# Counselor and advisor guide

Open https://stem-pathways-dashboard.vercel.app/. This dashboard explores Illinois degrees awarded at institutions. It does not track individual students or predict admission or employment outcomes.

1. Choose a field, degree level and award-year range. Add a city or institution-control filter when you want a local comparison. Reset restores the complete snapshot.
2. Overview shows annual totals, field and degree mixes, leading institutions and a summary. STEM fields sorts endpoint growth; unavailable means the baseline is missing, zero or the same year.
3. Institutions searches by name or city. The matching-records checkbox can be turned off to inspect the historical directory, including institutions without awards in the current selection.
4. Compare lets you choose up to three institutions. Compare exact totals in its table; individual trend charts have separate vertical scales.
5. Map shows institutions with positive matching awards. Search its list to locate a name. Open a name for the institution profile. Map coordinates use the latest directory record in the snapshot.
6. Open each chart's accessible table for exact values. Download matching CSV exports the records behind the current view. Copy selection link saves the view, filters and shortlist in the address. Print advising brief opens your browser's print dialog.

The latest computer science bachelor's preset selects the final snapshot year. Explore 2020 onward avoids crossing the CIP version change. Read About the data before presenting a conclusion, and confirm current programs directly with the institution.

Example: choose 2023, Computer & information sciences, Bachelor's, Chicago and Public. Overview shows 566 awards. Compare Chicago State University and University of Illinois Chicago to see 9 and 454 awards. Those counts describe award volume, not which college is better.
